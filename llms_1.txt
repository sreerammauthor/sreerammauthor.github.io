# The Loop Line

> The Loop Line is a debut literary/speculative novel in ten parts by Sreeram M, releasing October 19, 2026. It follows ten intertwined lives that share the same stretch of rail line — among them, a man and the person at the other end of it he stopped trying to explain years ago, a daughter returning to a house she barely recognizes, and a girl losing pieces of a day that hasn't happened yet — a book about repetition, memory, and the journeys people take again and again while waiting for the one that finally changes.
