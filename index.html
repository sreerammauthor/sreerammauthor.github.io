The premise

Every line has a loop in it somewhere.

Ilya has made this trip twice a year for eleven years — the same compartment, the same ticket folded back without a glance, the same novel he never finishes in his bag. Somewhere at the other end of the line is a man he stopped trying to explain a long time ago.

The Loop Line follows ten intertwined lives on the same stretch of track: a daughter returning to a house that's smaller than she remembered, a girl losing seconds to a day that hasn't happened yet, a driver logging his last shift before the route goes automatic, and others whose smallest choices ripple through years they'll never see.

Some journeys end. Some just fold back on themselves, again and again, until someone — the reader included — stops waiting for the arrival and starts paying attention to the ride.

Questions

Good to know

What is The Loop Line about?

A literary novel in ten parts about ten lives that share the same stretch of rail line — among them, a man and the person at the other end of it he stopped trying to explain years ago, a daughter returning to a house she barely recognizes, and a girl losing pieces of a day that hasn't happened yet. It's a book about repetition, memory, and the journeys we take again and again while waiting for the one that finally changes.

No — it's a standalone novel told in ten parts, each following a different character whose life intersects the same line.
